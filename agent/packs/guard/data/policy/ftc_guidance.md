# FTC consumer guidance

## Verification codes
Keep account verification codes to yourself. Someone who contacts you and asks for a code may use it to access your account. End the conversation and contact the company using a number or app you already trust.
Source: https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2024/03/whats-verification-code-why-would-someone-ask-me-it

## Unexpected calls
Caller ID can be faked. If an unexpected caller claims your account is at risk, hang up and contact the company through its official app or a number from a recent statement.
Source: https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2026/01/how-handle-unexpected-calls-claim-your-money-risk

## Suspicious texts
Do not open links in an unexpected text asking for account information. Contact the company through a website or phone number you know is real. Forward unwanted texts to 7726 (SPAM) and report suspected fraud at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
Source: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-and-report-spam-text-messages
